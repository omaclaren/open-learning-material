TRACKID3X3 BASKETBALL DATA AND DERIVATIVES

Source: TrackID3x3, Indoor subset, basket_S1T4_pre, frame 50 and supplied court
landmarks. Creators: Kazuhiro Yamada, Li Yin, Qingrui Hu, Ning Ding, Shunsuke
Iwashita, Jun Ichikawa, Kiwamu Kotani, Calvin Yeung and Keisuke Fujii (2025).
Dataset paper: https://doi.org/10.1145/3728423.3759400
Public archive: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1aWqMwQKr5xKMjqms7-raYluSlxPsGvwX
Dataset licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Course modifications include still extraction, crops, annotation/prediction
box overlays, labels, colour-channel views, geometric overlays and explanatory
layouts. The court helper converts landmark coordinates from centimetres to
metres and supplies a calculated image-to-court transformation. Detector
outputs and the explanatory figure styling were created for the course, not
provided as official results by the dataset authors. No endorsement is implied.
This website does not distribute the video or the practical-notebook package.
